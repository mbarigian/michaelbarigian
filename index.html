Executive narrative

From technical expert to enterprise transformation leader

My career began in distribution planning and interconnection, where I built a hands-on technical foundation in distribution system planning, DER interconnection, power system modeling, and analytics. Over time, that foundation evolved into leadership roles shaping how planning, interconnection, operations, technology, and regulatory policy come together.

Today, my work centers on regulatory strategy, grid policy, DER orchestration, energization, planning modernization, and the implementation of complex compliance requirements. I focus on creating alignment across engineering, operations, information technology, regulatory affairs, and external stakeholders.
